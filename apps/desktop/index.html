To sort your time into projects by itself, Open Staff reads the title of the window in front. macOS needs your OK for that.

Recorded: how much you type and click (counts only); the app, window title, site and page address in front; the folder you work in; whether you are on a call; Claude Code usage; and your commits.

Never recorded: what you type, your screen, sound, page contents, private browser windows, or your Claude conversations.

There is no timer to start. Time counts while you work and stops when you stop. You can close this window; Open Staff stays in your menu bar or tray.
